Questions

Before you join.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All-Access and Elite Coaching are month-to-month with 30 days' notice to cancel or downgrade. Drop-In has no commitment at all — pay per session.

I've never lifted before — is that a problem?

No. Strength Foundations is built for exactly that. Every new member starts with an assessment and a coach walks you through the big lifts before load gets added.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. All-Access and Elite members can freeze for up to 8 weeks per year for travel, injury, or anything else — just give us a heads up in advance.

What's included in Elite Coaching that isn't in All-Access?

Weekly 1:1 sessions with a coach of your choice, direct messaging for programming questions between sessions, and priority scheduling around competition dates.

Do you offer student or military discounts?

Yes — 15% off All-Access for students and active military with valid ID. Ask at the front desk or mention it when you book your first session.
