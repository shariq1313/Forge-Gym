Four ways in. Every one of them coached, programmed, and tracked from your first session.

Strength Foundations

Squat, bench, deadlift, press. This is where every member starts — coached technique on the big four lifts before load gets added, so the weight you chase later is built on a base that won't break down.

  • 3 sessions / week, 8-week block
  • Technique video review included
  • Baseline & retest lift numbers
Book an intro session

Powerlifting Club

Peaking cycles built around a real competition date, technical coaching on your squat-bench-deadlift, and a coach in your corner on meet day handling attempts and warm-ups.

  • 12-week peaking block
  • Meet-day coaching included
  • Requires Strength Foundations or equivalent
Book an intro session

Metabolic Conditioning

Short, brutal, effective. Rotating 40-minute circuits mixing kettlebells, sleds, rowers, and bodyweight work, built to torch fat and build a bigger engine without wrecking your lifts.

  • 40-minute class, any day
  • Scaled options every session
  • No lifting background required
Book an intro session

Open Gym + Recovery

Unsupervised floor time for members who already know their programming, plus guided mobility tools and a Sunday cooldown clinic for anyone carrying tightness into the week.

  • Full floor access during open hours
  • Mobility tools & foam rollers on-site
  • Sunday guided cooldown clinic
Book an intro session

How It Works

Four steps. No guesswork.

01

Assess

Baseline lifts, a mobility screen, and a conversation about what "strong" means to you.

02

Program

A training block built around your numbers, not a generic spreadsheet off the shelf.

03

Coach

Every session, a coach on the floor watching bar path — not counting reps from the front desk.

04

Progress

Retest every six weeks. Load goes up, or the program changes. Simple as that.
