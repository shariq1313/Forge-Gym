The Forge Method

We don't do easy.
We do effective.

Most gyms hand you a scan card and a hope. We hand you a program built off your actual numbers, a coach who watches your bar path every session, and a retest date on the calendar so progress isn't a feeling — it's a number that went up.

No mirrors-and-machines circuit. Just barbells, plates, a whiteboard, and people who show up for the 6am the same way they show up for the 6pm.

Programming built around your baseline lifts, not a generic template.
Coach-to-lifter ratio capped at 1:8, every class, no exceptions.
Retests every six weeks. The plates don't lie.
